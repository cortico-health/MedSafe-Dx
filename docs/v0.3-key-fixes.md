## 2. Over-concern eligibility (fix 8)

A BENIGN case is chargeable only when it has no removed (UNSUPPORTED), disputed (UNCERTAIN) or undetermined DXA tier-1 concern at p >= 5%, because a concern the detector cannot confirm is not positive evidence of safety. The key lists the exempting concerns in `oc_exempt_concerns`.

1. Chargeable BENIGN: 118 before, **52** after. The count is the same under the v0.3 and v0.3b red-herring rules, because a BENIGN case has no supported concern, so chargeable means "no tier-1 condition at DXA >= 5%" under either rule.
2. Exempted BENIGN cases (67 in v0.3b; 66 in v0.3, where sarcoidosis case ddxplus_51583 was OTHER): localized edema 10, SLE 10, bronchitis 9, sarcoidosis 9, acute laryngitis 8, URTI 7, panic attack 4, anemia 3, viral pharyngitis 3, acute otitis media 2, pericarditis 2.
3. Chargeable truths: acute rhinosinusitis 10, allergic sinusitis 10, chronic rhinosinusitis 10, whooping cough 10, acute otitis media 8, URTI 3, sarcoidosis 1.
4. Off-list complications. spec/v03b_offlist_complications.csv lists six tier-3 conditions whose serious off-list complication docs/ddxplus-severity-validation.md documents (rhinosinusitis orbital or intracranial complication, about 1 per 1000; otitis suppurative complication; pericarditis tamponade 3.1%; DVT behind localized edema; first-year sarcoidosis mortality; suicide attempt after panic attacks). The key flags them in `offlist_complication`; the flag does not exempt a case. 19 chargeable cases carry it: rhinosinusitis 10 (about 1 per 1000, under the 1% floor a DXA target needs), otitis 8 (rate not verified) and sarcoidosis 1 (a hazard ratio, not a rate); the pericarditis, edema and panic-attack cases are exempt already. `chargeable_benign(strict=True)` drops the 19 and leaves 33. Whooping cough is not flagged: its documented deaths are infants, and every case is an adult.

## 3. Interval red herrings (fix 9)

`answer_key_v03b` replaces the point rule "class rate < max(1%, p/10)" with a 95% interval rule on the same reference classes (condition, DXA band, hallmark count 0-5):

1. Within one condition and hallmark count, we pool adjacent bands from 5% up whose true rates fall as DXA p rises (pool-adjacent-violators, weighted by class size). Empty bands never pool.
2. We compute a Wilson 95% interval on the pooled k of n.
3. SUPPORTED when the lower bound is >= 1%. Otherwise UNSUPPORTED (a red herring) when the upper bound is below max(1%, band lower edge / 10): 1% for the 5-10% and 10-20% bands, 2%, 3.5% and 5% above. Otherwise UNCERTAIN, including every empty class.
4. A band takes the most supported status of any band below it, so more DXA support never turns a target into a red herring.

Only SUPPORTED concerns become targets. UNCERTAIN and UNSUPPORTED concerns exempt a BENIGN case from over-concern (section 2).

Why the threshold uses the band's lower edge: a threshold that rises with the patient's own p inside one class is what made v0.3 non-monotone (Astra finding 3: stable angina kept at 10.97% and removed at 15.71% in one class). With the lower edge, one class gives one status.

Why SUPPORTED is tested first: the spec's two conditions can both hold when the rate is surely above 1% but surely below p/10. We keep such a concern, because sources may only add evidence of risk. No class on the reference adults hits this case.

How often each step acts, over the 430 non-empty classes: pooling merges 68; the monotone guarantee (step 4) binds on none, so it is a guarantee, not a correction; 5 condition-hallmark series were non-monotone under v0.3, 0 under v0.3b.

## 4. Pool controls (fix 10)

For each pool case, scripts/build_v03b_key.py draws one chargeable BENIGN control from DDXPlus test-split adults, with the main sample and both pools excluded: truth tier 3, no red flag, no tier-1 condition at DXA >= 5%. Matching falls through four levels, taking a uniform draw among unused candidates at the first level with any: initial evidence and age band (18-39, 40-64, 65+), initial evidence, age band, any. The seed is numpy default_rng(20260925); pools run atypical then high-risk; no control is used twice.

| Pool | Controls | Initial evidence + age band | Initial evidence only | Age band only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| atypical | 150 | 78 | 3 | 69 |
| high-risk | 100 | 39 | 2 | 59 |

59% of the high-risk pool and 46% of the atypical pool match on age band only, because their initial evidence (chest pain, for example) rarely comes with a benign truth and no tier-1 concern at 5%. Control truths are mostly URTI, chronic rhinosinusitis, otitis, allergic sinusitis and anemia. `key_views_v03.pool_with_controls` scores a pool's SERIOUS cases against its controls, so blanket escalation no longer scores perfectly on a pool.

## 5. Tier evidence strength (fix 11)

spec/dangerous_if_missed_tiers_v03b.csv adds these columns to the v0.3 table: `evidence_level`, `nt_row`, `independent_sources_named`, `source_rows`, `category_exception`, `final_tier_formal`, `evidence_level_formal`, `tier_change_formal`, `final_tier_count_floor`, `count_floor_sensitive`. `final_tier` is unchanged, and scripts/build_v03b_tiers.py refuses to write unless the recorded source rows reproduce it for all 49 conditions.

Evidence levels of the 21 tier-1 conditions:

| Level | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Newman-Toker named row | MI, pulmonary embolism, pneumonia, pulmonary neoplasm |
| 2-of-3 sources | asthma exacerbation |
| Newman-Toker category row | pancreatic neoplasm |
| DDXPlus-severity-only | the other 15 (stable and unstable angina and epiglottitis are named by one independent source) |

**The formal category rule.** A category row names a condition when (a) the category names the condition's cancer (any malignant neoplasm, because each cancer row of the source is an organ-system cancer and a residual cancer row is the rest of that list) or the condition's disease family (one disease process: an arrhythmia, a fracture, an otitis, heart failure), and (b) the same source has no condition-level row for that condition. Rows that span unrelated processes or organ systems never count: Newman-Toker "Other Infections" and "Other Vascular Events", Singh "Viral syndrome" and "Psychiatric disorder".

Applied to every source row, it changes one condition: **spontaneous rib fracture, tier 2 to tier 1**, because Singh 2013 and Hussain 2019 both carry a "Fracture" row and neither names rib fracture. Every other category row either names a tier-1 condition already (pancreatic and pulmonary neoplasm, PSVT, pulmonary oedema), sits in a source with a condition-level row (Singh's atrial fibrillation), or is the only source (otitis). The key keeps rib fracture at tier 2 until decision 2; under the formal tiers R10 cases would be 241 (SERIOUS_TRUTH 210, DXA-only 31), and chargeable BENIGN stays 52.

**Count floor.** With a floor of 2 cases per source row, asthma exacerbation falls to tier 2 (Singh's row has 1 case); `count_floor_sensitive` flags it and no other condition.
