# v0.2 decoder audit

Date: 2026-09-23. Scope: `inference/symptom_decoder.py`, which turns DDXPlus evidence codes into the text the model reads. Method: we decoded all 504 evidence-and-value codes that occur in the DDXPlus test split, compared each English string with the question and the French original in `data/ddxplus_v0/release_evidences.json`, and counted how many of the 470 cases in `data/test_sets/eval-v02-adult.json` carry each defect. Tests: `evaluator/tests/test_v02_inputs.py`.

## How the versions coexist

`decode_symptoms_versioned(codes, version, sex)` takes `version="v0"` or `version="v02"`. The v0 functions are unchanged, byte for byte (we diffed their output on all 504 codes before and after the change), so the v0 test sets still decode to the prompts the published v0 runs saw. Prompt v4 uses v0, and prompt v5 uses v02 (`--prompt-version v5` in `inference/run_inference.py`). Every v5 prediction records `decoder_version`.

## Not fixed: listed as limits

These come from DDXPlus's generator or knowledge base, not from the decoder. A decoder that rewrote them would invent clinical content that the case does not carry.

| # | Defect | Cases | Why we leave it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Obstructive jaundice is encoded as a skin lesion: "yellow" colour, over 1 cm, in the epigastrium, with itching and raised scores | 9 (pancreatic neoplasm) | Only the colour says jaundice. Renaming it "jaundice" would be our diagnosis, and the lesion size, site and height would still describe a lesion. The paired "pale stools and dark urine" line is present and correct |
| 2 | An inguinal-hernia bulge is encoded as a pale, raised skin lesion over 1 cm in the iliac fossa | 8 (inguinal hernia) | Same reason; DDXPlus's differential then adds anaphylaxis and scombroid for these patients (docs/failure-modes/missed-escalation.md) |
| 3 | The patient's recorded sex contradicts a genital site (fix 15 rewrites the site, not the patient) | 8 | We do not change the sex field; a female hernia patient with genital-area pain is plausible (labial pain) |
| 4 | Antecedent codes follow condition priors, so implausible histories appear, such as COPD in a young pneumothorax patient or immunosuppression in 60% of adult viral-pharyngitis patients | many | Changing them would change the case. They also drive the NTS modifiers, which fire on 60-94% of adult patients with pneumonia, asthma, bronchitis, influenza and viral pharyngitis in the test split |
| 5 | Some symptoms are filed as history (`is_antecedent`), for example "Feel anxious" and "Regularly drink coffee or tea" | many | The split between Chief Complaints and Medical History comes from DDXPlus's `is_antecedent` flag; the text is still correct |
| 6 | Questions turned into fragments by stripping "Do you" and "Have you": "Ever had a pericarditis", "Feel anxious", "Your skin much paler than usual", "Are your symptoms worse when lying down ..." | most cases | The meaning survives, and rewriting about 150 strings by hand risks new errors without evidence that the fragments mislead. Candidate for a later pass with a reviewer |
| 7 | No duration, vital signs or examination | all | DDXPlus has none. Prompt v5 says so in its constraints |

## Checks

1. `test_every_test_split_code_decodes` decodes all 470 sampled cases with v02 and fails on any unknown code or any of the v0 strings "Recent travel to N", "Sudden pain onset", "Gradual pain onset", "NA", "nowhere", "palace", "heartbreaking", "whooping" and "Feel pain somewhere".
2. `TestDecoderV0Kept` pins the v0 strings and the v4 prompt, so a future edit cannot change v0 by accident.
3. The v02 text is longer: 753 characters of findings per case on average against 679 for v0, mainly from the scale labels.
