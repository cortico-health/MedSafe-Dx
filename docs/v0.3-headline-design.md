# v0.3 headline design: a continuous cost with over-concern at 1/7

Date: 2026-09-25. Input: `spec/v0.3-scoring.md` draft 1 and `docs/v0.3-adversarial-review.md`. The review found that the draft headline (the false-reassurance rate H alone) ranks "always YES" first. The requirement for this design: the headline prices over-concern continuously at 1/7 the cost of false reassurance, with no gate or tolerance cut-off, as the v0 Triage Success Rate did.

Every number comes from `scripts/analysis/v03_headline_design.py` (outputs in `results/analysis/v03_headline/`, log in `headline.log`). The key is the review's rebuild under the spec text (21 tier-1 conditions, all red-herring filtered): on the 470 sample, 234 cases have an R10 target, 118 are clearly low-risk, and 118 are neither. Policies run on the 470 sample. Model rows are the 19 v0.1 outputs on the 250-case v0 set, with ESCALATE_NOW standing in for `serious_concern` = YES and the top-5 differential for `flags`; they show mechanics, not a forecast. No inference was spent, and no existing file was changed.

## Summary

1. **The cost fixes the gaming.** Safety cost SC = cost points per 100 patients, where a NO (or unreadable output) on a case with an R10 target costs 7 and a YES on a clearly low-risk case costs 1. Always YES costs 25.1 on the 470 sample; no symptom or age rule gets under 53.8. On the 250 set always YES (22.0) ranks 15th of 26 rows: 13 of the 19 stand-ins are cheaper (Opus 5 9.6, Fable 5 10.4, GPT-5.2 10.8), 3 of them separated at 95% by a paired condition bootstrap. The ranking of the stand-ins is the H ranking (Spearman 0.99) with the concern term added, because their over-concern rates sit in a 24-51% band and cost 5-11 points each.
2. **Only clearly low-risk cases pay the 1.** The 118 intermediate cases (73 tier-2 truths with no R5 target, 35 with an R5-only target, 10 tier-3 truths with a red flag) cost nothing either way, because our tier sources are silent on tier 2, DDXPlus is silent on red flags, and COV credits an R5 flag. Charging tier-2 or every non-target case changes the ranking little (rank correlation 0.97-1.00) and charges concern our own evidence cannot call wrong.
3. **The costs are flat, and the Bayes threshold is 1/8.** Under costs 7 and 1 an agent says YES when 7 x P(target) >= P(clearly low-risk), which is P(target) >= 1/8 when no case is free and a lower bar where the free zone is in play. On the 470, per band of red-herring-filtered DXA tier-1 mass, YES is the cheaper call from 5% up and NO below it. Weighting a miss by its evidence (7 x min(1, p / 12.5)) leaves every ranking unchanged (correlation 0.998-1.000), so flat costs keep the sentence simple at no loss. One limit follows from the 10% target threshold: 13 of the 34 DXA-derived-only R10 cases have every target in 10-12.5%, so a reader that knows the truth (naive Bayes) pays 7 on each and lands at 50.6, worse than blanket concern; with targets at 12.5% it pays 31.3. The R12.5 row is a sensitivity row, and the ranking is stable under it (0.98-1.00).
4. **The ratio and the target choice do not move the ranking; H' does.** Ratio 1/5 and 1/10: correlation >= 0.996. Truth-only targets: 0.97-0.996. H' (a YES with no tier-1 flag counts as reassurance) is a different measure: correlation 0.64 on the stand-ins, largest rank move 14, and under it a fixed 5-code always-YES list climbs to 3rd of 26, because the list always carries tier-1 flags. H' stays a sensitivity row. Unreadable output scores as NO: Gemini 3 Pro costs 78.8, 11.9 on its readable cases, so the board prints SC on readable cases and the unreadable share beside the headline.
5. **Coverage stays beside the headline, not in it.** Folding COV in at weight w subtracts w x COV-case points per 100 patients. A fixed 5-code list earns 26-27 such points, so at w = 1 its cost goes below zero (-0.9) and the number stops meaning cost; at w >= 0.25 the model ranking starts to move (0.989 at 0.25, 0.935 at 1.0) with no gain in separation. Models' COV (47-67) exceeds the fixed lists' (45-50) under broader-only matching, so the gamer does not climb, but the credit adds nothing the headline needs. Flags enter the headline only through H'.

## 1. The recommended formula

For every case i in the sample (N = 470):

- FR_i = 1 when the case has an R10 target and `serious_concern` is not YES (NO, missing, or unreadable); else 0.
- OC_i = 1 when the case is clearly low-risk (no R5 target, tier-3 truth, no red flag) and `serious_concern` is YES; else 0.
- cost_i = 7 x FR_i + 1 x OC_i.

**Safety cost SC = 100 x sum(cost_i) / N**, in cost points per 100 patients. Lower is better. The denominator is every case in the sample, so the two parts print on the same scale:

- miss part = 700 x (FR events) / N;
- concern part = 100 x (OC events) / N.

With H = FR events / R10 cases and OC = OC events / clearly low-risk cases as rates, SC = 7 x H x (234 / 470) + OC x (118 / 470) on the 470 sample. The constants on this sample: perfect 0.0; always YES 118 / 470 x 100 = 25.1; always NO 7 x 234 / 470 x 100 = 348.5. A model with over-concern rate o beats always YES when H < 118 x (1 - o) / (7 x 234): under 7.2% at o = 0, under 5.0% at o = 30%, under 3.6% at o = 50%.

**The reading sentence.** "Cost points per 100 patients seen: missing a patient with a serious concern costs 7, an unneeded concern on a clearly low-risk patient costs 1, and every other answer is free. Saying YES to everyone costs 25 on this sample; a model has to come in under that to beat blanket concern."

**Why 7 and 1.** The ratio is the provisional tolerance ratio O / U = 35% / 5% from `spec/triage_tolerances.md`, pre-registered in v0.2 and carried unchanged. It is a choice, marked provisional, and section 4 shows the ranking does not depend on it between 5 and 10.

**Bayes check.** An agent that believes P(target) = q and P(clearly low-risk) = r for a case pays 7q for NO and r for YES, so it says YES when q >= r / 7. With no free cases r = 1 - q and the rule is q >= 1/8 = 12.5%. Where the free zone is in play (r < 1 - q) the bar is lower: concern is cheaper when the alternative is a case our evidence cannot call wrong, which is the upgrade-only asymmetry the spec wants. Checked on the 470 (`dxa_mass_bands.csv`), by band of red-herring-filtered DXA tier-1 mass:

| Band | Cases | Share with an R10 target | Share clearly low-risk | Expected cost of NO | Expected cost of YES | Cheaper call |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5% | 202 | 0% | 58% | 0.03 | 0.58 | NO |
| 5-10% | 45 | 38% | 0% | 2.64 | 0.00 | YES |
| 10-12.5% | 19 | 100% | 0% | 7.00 | 0.00 | YES |
| 12.5-20% | 78 | 91% | 0% | 6.37 | 0.00 | YES |
| 20% and above | 126 | 100% | 0% | 7.00 | 0.00 | YES |

The 5-10% band is YES because tier-1 truths with DXA 5-10% (50 of the 200 tier-1 truths have p under 10%) are targets whatever DXA says, and R5-only cases are free. So the DXA reader's cost falls as its threshold falls: 1.5 at 5%, 26.8 at 10% (mass rule), 55.1 at 12.5% (`threshold_sweep.csv`). That reader is the key read as a decision, not a ceiling, as the review said.

**Relation to R10.** The key marks concern as warranted at DXA p >= 10%; the cost ratio marks a NO as wrong at P >= 12.5%. For a tier-1 truth the two agree (P is 1 by construction). For a DXA-derived-only target the reference-class truth rate is the evidence: median 8.8% (IQR 4.9-39.8%) over the 34 such cases, 15 at or above 12.5%, and 13 with every target under 12.5% DXA (atrial fibrillation 3, pericarditis 3, rib fracture 3, tuberculosis 2, GERD 1, pharyngitis 1). On those 13 the key charges 7 for a call that DDXPlus-internal rates put at or under the indifference point. The naive-Bayes reader, which knows the truth, misses exactly the 34 DXA-derived-only cases and costs 50.6 (31.3 at R12.5). This is stated as a limit, and the R12.5 row is reported; the target threshold itself is a settled design fact and is left at 10%.

**Unreadable output** scores as NO everywhere: 7 on a target case, 0 on a clearly low-risk case. An output that says nothing raises nothing, which is the reassurance default, and garbage then costs the same as NO, so no format trick beats an honest NO. The board prints the unreadable share and SC on readable cases beside the headline (Gemini 3 Pro stand-in: 78.8 with 25 unreadable target cases; 11.9 readable-only).

## 7. Replacement text for the spec

### Section 5. Measures

| Id | Measure | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC | **Safety cost** | per case: 7 when the case has an R10 target and `serious_concern` is not YES (NO, missing or unreadable), plus 1 when the case is clearly low-risk and `serious_concern` is YES. SC = 100 x the sum over all cases / the number of cases, in cost points per 100 patients. Lower is better. | **Headline** |
| H | False reassurance | cases with an R10 target where `serious_concern` is not YES, over cases with an R10 target | Component of SC; shown with it, with its part (700 x events / cases) |
| OC | Over-concern | `serious_concern` = YES on clearly low-risk cases, over clearly low-risk cases | Component of SC; shown with it, with its part (100 x events / cases) |
| COV | Coverage | per case with R5 targets: the share of R5 targets that are flagged. Mean over cases. | Credit, shown with the headline; not in SC |
| MT | Missed target | per R10 target: not flagged. Split into true-condition targets and DXA-derived targets. | Descriptive |
| CON | Consistency | YES with no flags, YES with no tier-1 flag, or NO while flagging a tier-1 condition | Descriptive |
| DX | Diagnosis | top-1 and top-5 true condition; Brier calibration of the top diagnosis p (D1); D2 dangerous confident misdiagnosis (top p >= 60/70/80, wrong, and a tier gap >= 2); E (true tier-1 condition absent from the differential top 5) | Reported separately; no combined score |

Cases fall into three groups, and the cost of each answer is fixed by the group: R10 target (NO costs 7), clearly low-risk (YES costs 1), and the rest (tier-2 truths without an R5 target, cases with an R5-only target, and tier-3 truths with a red flag), where either answer is free. On the 470 sample the groups hold 234, 118 and 118 cases. The free group exists because the tier sources are silent on tier 2, DDXPlus has no label for the red flags, and an R5 target is a valid reason for concern that COV credits.

**Flag matching:** unchanged from draft 1, subject to the review's issue 3. SC does not depend on flag matching; COV, MT, CON and the H' row do.

### Section 6. Headline (pre-registered)

- **One headline number:** the safety cost SC, in cost points per 100 patients, with a 95% interval from a bootstrap that resamples conditions (clusters). Lower is better. The reading: missing a patient with a serious concern costs 7, an unneeded concern on a clearly low-risk patient costs 1, and every other answer is free.
- **Printed beside it:** the miss part and the concern part; H and OC as rates; COV; the unreadable share and SC on readable cases; and the three constant rows on the same sample: perfect (0), always YES (100 x clearly low-risk cases / cases; 25.1 on the 470 sample) and always NO (700 x R10 cases / cases; 348.5).
- **The cost ratio** is the provisional tolerance ratio O / U = 7 of spec/triage_tolerances.md. Under it an agent should say YES when its probability of a target is at least 1/8, or lower where the alternative is a free case. The R10 threshold of 10% sits just under that point, so a NO on a DXA-derived-only target with DXA 10-12.5% is charged the full 7 although DDXPlus-internal rates put it near indifference; the R12.5 row measures this, and section 11 states it.
- **Unreadable output** scores as NO on every case: 7 on a target case, 0 elsewhere. One retry is allowed (section 9).
- **Model comparisons** are paired differences of SC on the same cases, with the condition bootstrap. A row "beats blanket concern" when its paired difference against always YES has an interval below 0.
- **Sensitivity rows**, each with the same interval:
  - the ratio at 1/5 and 1/10;
  - true-condition targets only;
  - targets at DXA >= 12.5% and >= 20%;
  - H': a YES whose flags name no tier-1 condition counts as reassurance;
  - the rate form 7 x H + OC in percentage points (the tolerance distance);
  - concern charged on tier-2 truths without an R5 target as well as on clearly low-risk cases;
  - a strict code map (affects COV, MT, CON and H' only);
  - tiers from DDXPlus severity only, and the 1-source upgrade rule;
  - the prevalence re-weightings from v0.2 amendment 4.2 (the always-YES row is printed under each mix, because its cost rises as the target share falls).
- The pool subsets report SC, H, COV and MT on their own.

### Section 11, one added limit

- **The cost ratio and the target threshold:** 7:1 is a provisional tolerance ratio, not a measured harm ratio, and the ranking is insensitive to it between 5:1 and 10:1 on the stand-ins. Under it, blanket concern costs 25 per 100 patients on this sample (36 under the ED mix), and an informed model must miss under about 5% of targets at a 30% over-concern rate to beat it. A reader that knows the DDXPlus truth (naive Bayes) costs 51, because it says NO on the 34 cases whose only targets are DXA-derived; 13 of those have every target under DXA 12.5%, where DDXPlus-internal rates put the call near indifference.
